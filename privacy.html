Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Caché is made by Denis Dule. Contact: legal@dulelabs.com

What is collected

  • Usage data: app events such as onboarding steps and answers, links saved (counted in ranges), and paywall views, with a random ID made by Caché, your Caché Pro status and device and app versions. Used to improve the app.
  • Purchase data: your subscription’s plan, price, dates and App Store country, with the same random ID. Used to unlock Caché Pro.

Neither includes your links or anything you type, is linked to your identity, or is used for tracking or advertising. Your library stays on your device and, with iCloud Sync, in your private iCloud account.

Who receives it

Usage data goes to PostHog and purchase data to RevenueCat, which process it for Caché, possibly in the United States. Apple handles payments. Websites you save are contacted to load their previews.

Retention and your rights

Data is kept only as long as needed for these purposes. Turn off usage data in Settings › Privacy › Share Anonymous Usage Data. To access or delete your data, email the address above. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
