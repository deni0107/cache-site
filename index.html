Saving from another app

Tap Share on any post, video, product or page, then choose Caché. If Caché isn’t in the row of apps, scroll to the end, tap More, and turn Caché on.

Importing Safari tabs

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Caché Tabs, turn on Allow Extension, and set All Websites to Allow.
  2. In Caché, tap Import from Safari. Safari opens.
  3. Open Safari’s page menu, choose Caché Tabs, then tap Open.

Caché Pro

Restore a purchase, or see when your plan renews, in Caché › Settings › Caché Pro.

To cancel, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Caché. Your links stay yours after Pro ends, including private ones.
