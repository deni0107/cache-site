Send your tabs to Caché

Just two taps. This page is left out of the import.

  1. 1
    Tap the page menu at the left end of the address bar.
  2. 2
    Tap Caché Tabs in the menu that opens. Your open tabs go to Caché, and Caché opens to review them.
    Caché Tabs

Don’t see Caché Tabs? Turn it on in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions, and set All Websites to Allow. Your tabs stay open in Safari.
